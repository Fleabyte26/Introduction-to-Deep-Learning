Gradient descent: rolling downhill

The goal is the bottom of f(x) = x², at x = 0. The ball only feels the slope under it (2x) and steps against it. The learning rate decides how big each step is. Try the presets.

On this curve every step multiplies x by (1 − 2η). That one number explains everything: between 0 and 1 the ball creeps in from one side, between −1 and 0 it zig-zags but still settles, and below −1 (η above 1) each jump is bigger than the last.
